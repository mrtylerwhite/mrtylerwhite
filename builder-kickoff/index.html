THE METHOD

Four steps. Shown in the course.

01

Commit the appetite

Before anything opens in Figma, the team names the problem and the KPI, then agrees on what it's worth: one sprint, two, or more. Everyone's time is the investment. Scope has to fit it.

Lesson 2
02

Map it and make the call

Screenshots of the current flow go on the FigJam board. Everyone adds stickies for changes, constraints, edge cases and open questions. Then every sticky goes into in scope, maybe, or out. Not a 47-screen masterpiece. One decision the whole team can repeat back.

Lesson 3
03

Turn the transcript into a PRD

Record the meeting. The Claude skill turns the transcript into a PRD with success metrics, the bet, and the ROI case built in. A first draft you edit, not a blank doc you write.

Example: a checkout redesign that lifts conversions 5% on $50K ACV = $2.5K more per customer. Over 100 deals that's $250K. That's the kind of ROI case the skill builds from what your team actually said in the room.

04

Prototype for engineering

Engineering needs something to react to. The same transcript becomes a prototype prompt for Claude, so the next conversation starts from a working screen, not sticky notes. Ready to run yours? Get instant access for $97.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to bring my team?

No. You watch solo. It's a recorded course: you see the system run on an example project and learn the method to facilitate it with your team later.

Is this for designers who can't code?

No code required. The course is facilitation-first: slide deck, FigJam, Notion, and the Claude skill. If you can run a stakeholder conversation, you can run Builder Kickoff™.

Do I need FigJam or Notion?

Yes. The FigJam template and the Notion kit are core parts of the system, and Claude runs the PRD and prototype steps. Free tiers work for getting started. The kit walks you through setup step by step.

Is this just vibe coding?

No. Vibe coding is what happens when there's no structure, no intake, and no idea what you're actually trying to prove. You get impressive-looking garbage nobody can ship, and everyone's too polite to say so. This course isn't a prompt dump. It's a system.

How long do I have access?

It's yours to keep. Watch it now, then rewatch the module you need right before your next kickoff.

Is this beginner friendly?

It assumes you have shipped at least one digital product and know your way around a stakeholder conversation. If you have shipped dozens, it will probably change how you start every new one.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No prep required. I bring the example project. You press play, watch the walkthrough, and leave with the complete Builder Kickoff System™.
